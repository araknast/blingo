How this works

A simulated repository in which superagents keep a fixed number of agents busy. Each agent runs for 6 to 30 minutes, changes a few files (shared files like src/db/schema.ts 8% of the time), then goes through CI and review and is placed. The numbers are illustrative; the rules are blingo's.

  • Placement follows placeFork: home if it's visible, at the same epoch, and nothing since the fork started changed its files; otherwise the first bubble that neither it nor the home bubble touched those files in; otherwise a new bubble.
  • Patch queues: each bubble's Box applies its queue, then pushes. A bubble with a queue can't neighbour-merge.
  • Merge loop: one neighbour merge per repository at a time, nearest neighbour first. Merges fail when both sides changed the same files since they split.
  • Ferges, CI fixers and the 3-push limit match the app. Auto-ferge and ferge agents are always on, so a conflict is only a red line until the merge loop ferges it. There's no "Needs you" panel: nothing in the simulation acts on it.

Differences from the real map: it draws on a canvas instead of SVG, the dock shows at most 60 chips per group, and you can zoom out further than 25% so thousands of bubbles fit.
